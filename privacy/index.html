Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Wreckship (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates https://www.wreckship.me (the “Website”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how third-party vendors (including Google AdSense) use cookies, and the choices available to you.

For detailed information on managing cookie preferences or revoking consent, please visit our Cookie Policy or click Cookie Settings anytime.

Information we collect

Information you provide

You may choose to contact us by email. In that case, we may receive your name, email address, message, and any information you choose to include in your inquiry.

Information collected automatically & Maritime Data Analytics

When you interact with Wreckship, we or our analytics service providers automatically collect limited technical usage data to operate, maintain, and optimize our 3D interactive map experience. This includes:

  • IP address and coarse geographic region
  • Browser type, device model, operating system, and WebGL rendering capabilities
  • Vessel and incident search queries, category filter selections, and timeline year ranges
  • Pages viewed, referral URLs, date/time stamps, and system performance or error logs

This data helps us evaluate map render performance, improve wreck database search responsiveness, and understand global audience interest across historical maritime categories.

Wreckship uses Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to serve advertisements when you visit our Website.

  • Use of Cookies by Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to Wreckship or other websites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to Wreckship and/or other sites across the web.
  • Consent Mode v2: We implement Google Consent Mode v2 to respect your privacy preferences before advertising technologies execute. Non-essential cookies and ad personalization technologies are defaulted to denied unless explicitly granted by you.

Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You have full control over targeted advertising and cookie consent. You can opt out of personalized advertising through any of the following methods:

  • Google Ad Personalization: Opt out of Google’s personalized ads by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry Opt-Out Portals: Opt out of targeted advertising from participating third-party ad networks via the Digital Advertising Alliance (AboutAds Choices) or European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (YourOnlineChoices).
