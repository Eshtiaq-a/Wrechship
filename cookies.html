Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Wreckship uses cookies and similar technologies on https://www.wreckship.me.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help a website function securely, remember preferences, understand usage, and support advertising.

Cookies we use or may use

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for core Website functionality, security, and basic preferences. They cannot generally be disabled through our Website.

Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand traffic, performance, and how visitors use Wreckship. They may collect aggregated or pseudonymous usage information.

Advertising cookies

If advertising is enabled, advertising partners such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to display ads, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, and personalize advertising where permitted by law.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this Website and/or other websites. You can manage ad personalization through Google’s Ads Settings: https://www.google.com/settings/ads

Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also customize or revoke non-essential cookie consent at any time on Wreckship by clicking Cookie Settings.

Blocking some cookies may affect certain Website features or functionality.

Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.
